1. About Sajha Seema

Sajha Seema is an independent civic information prototype intended to demonstrate how publicly available, non-sensitive information and community concerns might be organized for public understanding.

It is not a Government of Nepal website, government agency, official reporting channel, or emergency service. It does not claim government approval or affiliation.

2. Acceptable use

When using this website, you agree not to:

  • Submit knowingly false, misleading, or fabricated information.
  • Harass, threaten, or target individuals or communities.
  • Publish private personal information without permission.

  • Attempt to disrupt, damage, or misuse the website or its services.
  • Use the website to encourage discrimination or violence.

3. Information accuracy

Information displayed on this prototype may be incomplete, outdated, illustrative, or unverified. Demo records are examples, not confirmed real-world events.

A displayed status does not guarantee that an incident has been independently confirmed. Users should consult appropriate official sources for authoritative information.

4. Community submissions

Any submission interface on this prototype is for demonstration unless a page clearly explains that a working submission service has been established.

A browser-side preview does not mean a report has been sent to an authority, stored in a secure database, or reviewed by an official organization.

5. Public information and safety

Do not submit information that could expose people to harm or reveal sensitive security details. This includes private identities, exact vulnerable locations, suspected routes or gaps, live movements, and confidential documents.

Public-facing information should be limited to appropriate, non-sensitive administrative and community-level details.
