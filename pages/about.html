What this project currently is

A student-built, independent prototype for demonstrating a possible public information platform. It is not a government database, official reporting channel or operational monitoring system.

PROTOTYPE
OUR PURPOSE

Why Sajha Seema exists

Accessible information

Make relevant public documents and institutional information easier for people to discover and understand.

Responsible participation

Explore ways for people to express general civic concerns without exposing private or sensitive information.

Transparent review

Demonstrate how information can be labelled according to its source, review status and verification limitations.

Public awareness

Encourage informed discussion based on reliable public information rather than rumours or unsupported claims.

OUR PRINCIPLES

What guides this project

01 — ACCURACY

Evidence over assumptions

Information should be traceable to credible public sources. Unverified submissions must never be presented as confirmed facts.

02 — PRIVACY

People before data

Personal identities, private documents and sensitive location details should not be collected or publicly exposed.

03 — INDEPENDENCE

Clear project identity

The project must clearly identify itself as independent and must never imply official government endorsement.

04 — ACCOUNTABILITY

Honest limitations

Data sources, review decisions, corrections and limitations should be explained clearly to visitors.
