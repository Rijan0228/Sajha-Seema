OUR APPROACH

Methodology & standards

How Sajha Seema proposes to organize public information, distinguish verified sources from unverified submissions, and protect people from unnecessary exposure.

A proposed framework, not an operational verification service

The procedures described here are design principles for a future responsible platform. This prototype does not independently investigate or officially verify reports.

PROTOTYPE
THE PROCESS

How information should be handled

STEP 01

Identify the source

Prefer official public documents, published notices, institutional websites and other traceable sources.

STEP 02

Review the information

Check whether the source supports the statement being made. Do not treat an unsupported claim as established fact.

STEP 03

Apply a clear label

Explain whether information is a demonstration, awaiting review, supported by a public source or archived.

STEP 04

Maintain transparency

Make corrections and limitations understandable. Keep a clear distinction between public records and opinions.

REVIEW LABELS

Understanding record status

Source reviewed Source checked

A reviewer has checked the cited public source for the specific information described. This does not mean a government agency has officially confirmed it.

Awaiting review Not confirmed

Information has not completed the proposed review process. It must not be presented as a confirmed fact.

Closed / archived Inactive record

A record is no longer active in the demonstration workflow. Its archived status does not establish whether its original claim was true or false.

DATA PRINCIPLES

What should and should not be published

Appropriate public information

  • Public institutional documents.
  • General administrative information.
  • Broad province-level summaries.
  • Clearly attributed public notices.
  • Non-sensitive corrections.

Information to exclude

  • Sensitive infrastructure coordinates.
  • Alleged crossing points or security gaps.
  • Private identities and contact details.
  • Movement tracking of people or vehicles.
  • Confidential or restricted documents.
CORRECTIONS

Handling mistakes responsibly

CORRECTION PRINCIPLE

Accuracy matters more than appearance

If a published demonstration record contains an error, the responsible maintainer should review the correction, update the information where appropriate and explain any significant change. Unresolved disagreements should not be presented as settled facts.

LIMITATIONS

What this platform cannot promise

Important limitations

Sajha Seema is not an official reporting channel, emergency service, law-enforcement platform, government database or independent fact-investigation organization. It cannot guarantee that information is complete, current or officially recognized.
