Privacy policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit Sajha Seema, how the current prototype works and what its privacy limitations are.

Privacy by design

The current website is a static GitHub Pages prototype. It does not operate a private reporting database or secure account system.

POLICY INFORMATION

About this policy

Project: Sajha Seema
Website: rijan0228.github.io/Sajha-Seema/
Policy version: 1.0
Last updated: 10 November 2026

Sajha Seema is an independent civic information prototype. It is not operated by or affiliated with the Government of Nepal.

01 — INFORMATION

Information we collect

Information you enter

The current demonstration form allows you to enter a general category, a province and a short description. Its preview is generated inside your browser. The current form does not send that message to Sajha Seema or save it to a project database.

Technical information

GitHub Pages and other services involved in delivering the website may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request timestamps and security logs under their own policies. The project does not control all such infrastructure processing.

Information we do not request

The prototype does not require an account, password, identity document, phone number or precise location to browse its pages. Do not enter these details into demonstration fields.

02 — PURPOSE

How information is used

The current prototype uses information entered into the demo form only to create a temporary on-screen preview. It is not submitted as an official complaint and is not transmitted to an authority.

Public demonstration records are fictional examples used to show how a future information registry might look.

If future versions introduce accounts, analytics, external submission services or persistent storage, this policy must be reviewed and updated before those features are introduced.

03 — STORAGE

Storage and retention

Browser activity

The current demonstration form does not intentionally store submitted messages in a project database or browser storage. Temporary page state may remain in memory while the page is open.

Hosting infrastructure

Website delivery and security logs may be retained by the hosting provider according to that provider's own policies and retention practices. Sajha Seema cannot promise a specific retention period for provider-controlled logs.

Future submissions

No future submission or reporting service should be introduced without a documented purpose, appropriate access controls, retention rules and a clear explanation of who can access the data.

04 — THIRD PARTIES

External services

This website currently loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from the Font Awesome CDN hosted by Cloudflare. Your browser may contact those providers to retrieve these resources.

GitHub Pages hosts the website. These external services may process technical information under their own privacy policies.

We do not claim that these providers are acting as data processors under an agreement with this independent prototype.

05 — SECURITY

Keeping information safe

The project follows a privacy-conscious design approach by avoiding unnecessary personal information in its current demonstration. However, a static public website cannot guarantee the security or confidentiality of information entered into third-party services.

Do not submit sensitive information

Never enter passwords, identity documents, private contact details, confidential records or security-sensitive locations into this prototype.

06 — YOUR CHOICES

Your privacy choices

  • You can browse the prototype without creating an account.
  • You can choose not to use the demonstration form.
  • You can control browser cookies and site data through your browser settings.
  • You can avoid loading optional external resources by using suitable browser controls or privacy tools.

Requests relating to information held by GitHub or other external providers must be directed to those providers under their own applicable policies.

07 — CHANGES

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features, hosting arrangements or information practices change. The updated version and revision date should be published on this page.
