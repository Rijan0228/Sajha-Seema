Cookies Policy

How cookies and similar technologies relate to this website.

Last updated: 10 November 2026 Independent prototype

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store in a visitor's browser. They can be used for functions such as remembering preferences, maintaining sessions, and measuring website usage.

2. Cookies used by this prototype

The current Sajha Seema prototype does not intentionally set first-party tracking or advertising cookies. It also does not currently include an analytics service or advertising tracker.

This may change if future versions introduce features that require cookies or similar technologies. Any such changes should be reflected in this policy.

3. Third-party services

This website may load resources from external providers, including Google Fonts and the Font Awesome content delivery network. GitHub Pages also provides the website hosting.

When your browser requests resources from these providers, they may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and request details. Their own privacy policies and practices apply to those requests.

4. Browser settings

Most browsers allow you to review, block, or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking cookies may affect features on some websites, although the current prototype is not designed to depend on first-party tracking cookies.

5. Local browser storage

Some websites use browser storage technologies such as localStorage or sessionStorage to remember information. The current prototype does not intentionally use persistent browser storage to save submitted community reports.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, visit the Sajha Seema GitHub repository .
