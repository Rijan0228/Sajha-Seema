Protect yourself and others

Responsible participation means sharing only information that is safe and appropriate for public review.

  • Use broad administrative areas only.
  • Refer to publicly available documents.
  • Separate personal observations from verified facts.
  • Do not include exact sensitive locations.
  • Do not upload private or identity documents.
  • Do not identify or accuse individuals.
If a matter requires official attention, contact the relevant competent authority through its published official channels.
TRANSPARENCY & ACCOUNTABILITY

Verification centre

A clear review framework helps prevent unverified submissions from being mistaken for established facts.

01

Received

A submission or public document is received for initial screening.

02

Safety review

Private details and unsafe content are excluded from publication.

03

Source check

Available public references are checked for authenticity and context.

04

Publication decision

A reviewer records whether the item can be safely published and with what qualification.

STATUS DEFINITIONS

What each label means

Awaiting review

Not yet assessed

The item has not completed the review process. It must not be treated as verified information.

Source reviewed

Reference checked

A reviewer has checked the cited public source. This does not automatically confirm every claim in a submission.

Closed / archived

No active review

The record has been closed or archived. Its history and reason should be documented where appropriate.

Review is not an official determination

Sajha Seema cannot establish legal facts, determine territorial boundaries, or replace decisions made by competent public authorities. Any public claim must be presented with careful context and source attribution.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Information should be understandable.

Sajha Seema is a proposed independent civic-tech project focused on responsible access to public information and transparent community feedback.

OUR PURPOSE

Make public information easier to find, understand and review.

The project concept brings together public documents, general administrative context and a carefully moderated feedback process. It aims to make information easier to navigate without exposing private individuals or sensitive infrastructure details.

Transparency

Explain where information comes from and when it was updated.

Responsibility

Use careful language and avoid turning allegations into facts.

Privacy

Minimize personal data and avoid publishing sensitive details.

Participation

Make public information and feedback processes easier to understand.

Independent project notice

Sajha Seema is not a government department, law-enforcement service, official border authority or emergency reporting channel. Its publication does not represent an official government position.

METHODOLOGY & GOVERNANCE

How information is handled

Trust depends on the process behind the information, not just the appearance of a dashboard.

01

Source selection

Prefer official public documents and datasets. Record the publisher, title, date and original link.

02

Context checks

Check what a source actually says, what it does not say, and whether it is current and relevant.

03

Privacy and safety screening

Exclude private identifying information, exact sensitive locations and details that could create safety risks.

04

Review labels

Clearly distinguish pending items, source-reviewed records and archived records. A source check is not an official determination.

05

Corrections

Provide a way to flag errors, add context or request removal of information that should not have been published.

06

Data updates

Display last-reviewed dates and archive outdated material rather than presenting it as current.

Privacy and data limitations

This GitHub Pages prototype is a public static website. It has no secure private submission backend, user accounts or protected database. Do not use it to collect confidential reports, personal information or sensitive documents.

Before any real reporting service is launched, it would need a documented privacy policy, secure backend, access controls, moderation procedures, retention rules and appropriate legal and institutional review.
